# Notice

ISGN Toolkit 2.0 is a research/application toolkit for recovering missing industrial mechanisms from partial physics and operational data.

- The toolkit does not prove that a recovered equation is the unique true physical law.
- `reference-sindy` is a small offline reference implementation, not official PySINDy.
- Official PySINDy and the external SymMatika bridge are optional runtimes and may be unavailable.
- `recover_missing_terms` uses residual sparse regression inspired by partial-physics decomposition ideas; it is not an implementation of Universal Differential Equations or APHYNITY.
- Observation qualification, backend agreement and completion certification are separate evidence layers.
- Predictive or neural models are not automatically Mechanism Models merely because they predict well.
- This release is suitable as an application-driven research baseline, not as independently certified safety/maintenance-critical production software.
